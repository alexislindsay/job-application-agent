## Canadian Pacific Railway (2005 - May 2013)
*Note: Roles often condensed on resumes to focus on Customer Service (2005-2011) and Accounting/Collections (2011-2013).*

**Role: Customer Service / Transportation Service / Operations Rep (2005 - 2011)**
- **Train Reporting & Workflow:** Managed inbound and outbound train queues for assigned geographic regions. Handled arrival and departure reporting at a car-by-car level using a proprietary mainframe system (TIES). Addressed exceptions when automatic AEI readers failed or had discrepancies.
- **Interchange Management:** Managed train interchanges with foreign railroads. Verified proper billing for train cars (including hazmat) by printing train lists from the mainframe. Identified cars missing billing and coordinated with interchanging railroads to resolve issues or sideline cars.
- **Customer Interaction & Car Ordering:** Communicated directly with industry customers to take orders for rail cars arriving in local servicing yards.
- **Waybilling & Order Entry:** Used proprietary systems to create waybills (routing labels containing origin, customer info, destination, weight, contents). Handled EDI waybill exceptions.
- **Hazmat Compliance:** Processed waybills for hazardous materials. Maintained strict compliance with US Federal Railroad Administration (FRA) standards, completing annual training and testing for hazmat transport. Verified billing for hazmat cars during interchanges.
- **Intermodal & Mainframe Operations:** Performed complex, manual marrying processes within the mainframe system to link multiple intermodal containers to individual rail cars.
- **Crew Operations & Work Orders:** Issued train documentation and work orders to engineering crews. Processed completed work orders manually based on conductor paperwork. Executed track moves and yard reorganization based on directions from yardmasters.
- **Cross-Border / Customs Operations:** Managed special procedures and specific waybilling for customs trains crossing the US-Canada border.
- **Peer Training:** Acted as a dedicated peer-to-peer trainer for new hires after their classroom training, teaching them highly customized, region-specific processes.
- **Tools Used:** Microsoft Office (Outlook, Excel), Mainframe command systems, proprietary digital fax software, PeopleSoft phone system.

**Role: Mail Clerk (2011 - 1 year)**
- Drove company vehicles to deliver interoffice documents and mail between the Minneapolis HQ, the St. Paul intermodal yard, and the St. Paul rail yard.
- Handled all incoming/outgoing mail, packages, large-scale mailings, FedEx, and UPS at headquarters.

**Role: Collector / Accounting (2011 - May 2013)**
- **B2B Collections:** Managed collections for assigned business accounts. Liaised with external Accounts Payable departments to secure payment for past-due invoices and resolve accounting errors.
- **Account Monitoring:** Actively monitored past-due invoice amounts at aggregate and individual customer levels using BI reports, ensuring invoices rarely exceeded 30 days past due.
- **Investigative Accounting & Revenue Recovery:** Investigated old, unassigned payments and overpayments sitting in the system. Successfully cleaned up legacy accounts, finding and correctly applying over $500,000 in revenue that was lost to system errors.
- **Manual Invoicing:** Printed and mailed physical invoices for a subset of legacy customers.
- **Tools Used:** SAP (customized), SAP Business Objects (BI / BOBJ) for reporting, Microsoft Excel, Outlook.

**Role: Service Center Manager (Dec 2013 - 2014)**
- **Locomotive Maintenance & FRA Compliance:** Managed the locomotive service center at the rail yard in Avon, IN. Ensured all maintenance operations strictly adhered to Federal Railroad Administration (FRA) regulations, including managing critical 92-day and 180-day locomotive inspection cycles.
- **Safety Leadership:** Championed a culture of safety among crews working around heavy equipment. Led daily 15-minute start-of-shift safety meetings, stretching routines, and safety point discussions. Successfully achieved a milestone of **180 days injury-free and incident-free**.
- **Union Team Management:** Directly managed and delegated work to a team of unionized contract employees. Navigated and ensured compliance with complex Collective Bargaining Agreements (CBAs) when assigning tasks and managing workflows.
- **Incident Investigation & Discipline:** Served as the company representative during union investigations and trials following injuries or rule violations. Evaluated incidents and determined appropriate disciplinary actions.
- **Train Readiness & Time Management:** Managed the "writing of trains" and in-between servicing (e.g., directing teams to clean locomotives, perform inspection services, and stock supplies). Operated under highly strict time designations (e.g., ensuring locomotives were ready 12 hours prior to departure).
- **Crisis Management:** Handled high-pressure logistical emergencies, such as dispatching mechanics to resolve sudden on-pad mechanical failures immediately prior to scheduled departures.
- **Coaching & Work Delegation:** Liaised with crews throughout shifts to delegate work, remove blockers, ensure proper resources, and verify safe working conditions.

**Role: Manager of Operations and Data Control (2014 - April 30, 2017)**
- **High-Volume Response:** Recognized as the #1 responder during peak volume, successfully handling over 1,000 locomotive calls in a single month.
- **Crisis & Derailment Management:** Acted as the central point of contact during major mainline derailments and collisions. Coordinated with emergency services, managed the dispatch of wrecking equipment, reported on car rerouting, and ensured strict compliance with FRA documentation requirements.
- **Executive Communication:** Managed real-time communications during high-stakes incidents, tailoring updates (via texts or calls) to senior executive leadership, including the Vice President of Mechanical.
- **Remote Technical & Mechanical Support:** Guided engineers through complex line-of-road mechanical failures (e.g., computer resets, tripped overdrives, reconnecting air hoses). Coordinated inspections and responses for broken axles and hot wheel detector alerts.
- **Positive Train Control (PTC) Pioneer:** Served as an early adopter and troubleshooter during the initial rollout of PTC software. Investigated system faults and false positives before the establishment of a dedicated PTC desk.
- **System Administrator & Tech Support:** Acted as the primary support and trainer for "Seymour," a highly customized Maximo-based inventory management system. Troubleshot errors and provided intuitive, customized coaching to all levels of staff, from field mechanics up to Shop Superintendents. 
- **Safety Reporting:** Authored and published the daily mechanical safety bulletin utilized across the entire network, tracking injury statistics and distributing critical safety protocols.
- **Cross-Network Coordination:** Liaised with field employees (both locomotive and car operations managers) and foreign railroad mechanical desks for cross-system troubleshooting and high-level coordination.
- **Resume Highlight:** Collaborated with internal stakeholders to improve processes and address operational incidents, reducing incident rates by 15% and improving productivity by 10%. Communicated progress reports to Senior Executive Leadership, demonstrating a 20% increase in overall operational efficiency.
